The Creative Coding Bookclub is a community for people interested in exploring creative coding through reading and hands-on challenges.

On 2025 we followed 'The Nature of Code' (2024) by Daniel Shiffman.

This event is for anyone - beginners and experienced coders equally welcomed!

Future events will take place at the Borough Road Gallery, part of London South Bank University, which kindly agreed to host us on a regular basis.

We'll meet on the last Thursday of each month - find out more about upcoming events at Luma!

If you post any bookclub-related content in social media feel free to mention us or tag the #CreativeCodingBookclub hashtag for us to see your posts!
